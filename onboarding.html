Welcome to PromptShield

You're protected. PromptShield quietly watches your AI messages and catches personal info before it leaves your hands.

100% local · no data ever leaves your browser
How it works
1
You type normally
Use any supported AI chat site just like you always do. PromptShield runs silently in the background.
2
PII is detected
Before your message sends, PromptShield scans it locally and highlights anything sensitive it finds.
3
You choose what to hide
A modal shows your message with detected items highlighted. Click any item to toggle it. Then send.

Privacy promise
🏠
Fully local
Detection runs entirely in your browser using regex patterns. No servers involved.
🚫
Zero storage
Detected PII is held in memory only while the modal is open. Nothing is saved.
🔒
Isolated modal
The review modal runs in a closed Shadow DOM — the AI site can't read what's inside it.
🧠
No learning
Your data is never used to train anything. Ever.
